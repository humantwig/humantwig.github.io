Passion Project: Terra

Terra or Terraform is a project I've had in mind for a while.
As an athlete and runner, I wanted to create a brand that incorporates my passion for trail running and sustainability within the apparel and outdoor industry. Taking inspiration from brands such as Salomon, Satisfy Running, Mount to Coast, and the New Zealand outdoor brand Kathmandu.
