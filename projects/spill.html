Overview statement regarding the scope and vision for this multimedia application or interface build.

SPILL is an interactive storytelling experience that takes users on a journey through different characters financial situations. The installation enables users to play a part in the story, acknowledging the various choices they make, and explaining why it's so important to take the topic of finance into an easy and transitional conversation.
